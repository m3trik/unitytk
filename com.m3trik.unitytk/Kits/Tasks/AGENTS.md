# unitytk/com.m3trik.unitytk/Kits/Tasks

**Role**: The Tasks kit: the builders that turn a plan's rows into a task's behaviors by composing the features (Interaction, Steps, RenderEffects, Indicators, Placement, Viewpoints, Panels). Editor composition alone; where code goes is [the package's map](../../AGENTS.md).

**Nav**: [← the package](../../AGENTS.md) · [← unitytk](../../../AGENTS.md) · [the kit's guide](../../../docs/tasks.md) · where its rows come from: [the sheet reader's rules](../../../unitytk/sheets/AGENTS.md)

## Rules

- **One builder a word.** The sheet's words (pythontk's template: `highlight`, `indicate`, `show`, `hide`, `set`, `take`, `return`, `use`, `play`, `button`, `select`, `answer`, `travel`, `picture`, `list`, `tick`, `note`) are the plan's behaviors and the kit's builders, registered by the word, with the kit's own `advance` beside them. A word is named for what its family does at its most general; a case of it (an arrow, a fade, a teleport, a press held) is a setting's value or the builder's detail, never a word or a builder of its own.
- **A behavior is a combination, never a one-off.** A row is a trigger, a requirement, an effect, a look and a place; a builder wires the feature that owns each through its action. A new word is built from the primitives the kit already builds; a builder is added only for a kind of primitive none covers, never for one setting's value.
- **A builder branches on its row's cells and settings** (its To, its `trigger`, its span), never on a word's name.
- **A setting belongs to its primitive**: one name, one meaning and one editor in every word, spelled as the sheet's template spells it (`when`, `trigger`, `hold`, `until`, `ring`, `state`, `speed`, `reply`, `color`, `size`, `style`, `fade`). It lives in the word's own group of `TaskSettings`, the member named as the word (`indicate.size`), beside the kit's own for that word; a row's setting resolves there by the sheet's name, and the window keeps an object's own value by the group's name. A builder takes no second name for one.
- **A setting resolves in one order**: the kit's default (`TaskSettings`), the manifest's, the row's Settings, an edit made in the Manifest window (`Manifest.Tune`); each level is shown where its value is.
- **Placing is the Placement feature's, never a word's setting.** The search for a first spot (stand-off, view angle, what an arrow merges with) runs once, where nothing is placed yet, and again only when a person resets it. A word exposes what a person dials in afterwards; no new search setting goes on a word or in `TaskSettings`.
- **The helper in the scene is the truth.** Anything placed (a viewpoint, an arrow, a picture's camera, a page's spot) is a named object a person moves like any other; a row's `where` names one an artist made, one entry a Target (an arrow's `where` is where its tip rests, pointing along it; one that draws its own mesh is the arrow; a travel's is where the user stands, facing its forward). A build reads a helper as it stands and never moves or turns one a person or the delivery placed; it makes one only where none exists. No new offset is kept in a file, and no position in a setting.
- **A cue is no part of what it points at**: an arrow the kit makes is left out of the fades and highlights above it (`RenderEffectsActions.Exempt`), so it shows while its target is hidden and keeps its own look while its target glows.
- **No behavior is known by its name.** What a row is, the kit reads from its traits (`[TaskBehavior]`: `ActsOn`, `Offers`, `EndsStep`, `Travels` and the rest), refined per row (`TaskBehaviors.Traits(action, step)`, `ITaskRowTraits`), and its builder's interfaces (`IStepGate`, per row: `TaskBehaviors.Gate(action, step)`; `ITaskPageContent`); never `behavior == "x"`.
- **Order is the plan's**: a step's user rows are open together and the step waits for all of them; what happens as a step starts or ends is the row's moment (its `when`, where its word may move); a row's position decides nothing else.
- **A shown thing has a span** (`step`, or until a named step starts), carried by the row that shows it (a picture's and a list's `until`, a travel's `ring`): no row takes it away.
- **A row a build cannot do says so**: `Check` returns what is wrong with its fix (a state the delivery lacks, a near trigger's Target inside its With, a span naming no later step); never a silent skip, a guess or a default that hides it.
- **A second build changes nothing, and a person's adjustment survives every build.**
- **A task is its settings file.** What a build names or keys (its top-level objects, a placed spot's key: `travel/<step>`, `picture/<step>`, `indicate/<step>/<path>`) goes by what stays across versions of the task's inputs: the settings file, a node's path under the delivery. Never a sheet's or a delivery's file name: a renamed sheet or a dated re-export is the same task.
- **The kit assumes no one task**: its defaults fit any project, a project's choices are its manifest's settings, and a client's name, path or content never enters the package.

**Proof**: the sample task built and played through as a user would (`TaskSampleTests`): `python -m pythontk ops run unity_package.compile`, then `python unitytk/tests/run_tests.py --edit-mode -k M3trik.Unitytk.Kits.Tasks`.
